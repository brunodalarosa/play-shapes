Here we'll define all the possible player journeys in the game.

### Playing a specific minigame
Game starts > Playground lobby online > Players join > Host selects a minigame and presses **Start** > [[Pre-minigame screen]] > every participant in the selected round is ready > [[Minigame Journey]] > results > return to the lobby.

A phone that already had onboarding open before **Start** may finish joining during the ready screen. It joins the current round unready. The ready screen has no join QR, and onboarding cannot be opened for the first time during this phase. Joining closes when the minigame begins. The host can cancel back to the lobby.
