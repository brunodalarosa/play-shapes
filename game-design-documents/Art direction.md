Play Shapes takes place in a world of playful imagination. Familiar real-world settings and objects mingle with toy-like figures and paper crafted characters that have come to life. Keep this idea consistent across menus, minigame environments, characters, effects, and other visual elements.

## Visual vocabulary

Every visual element should connect clearly to at least one of these sources:

- Toys
- Paper
- Papercraft
- Pencil drawings

These sources can be combined. Use their materials, shapes, colors, construction, or behavior to make an element feel at home in the same imaginative world. When proposing or creating a visual, be able to identify which source or sources guide it.

## Rendering style

Favor playful, stylized visuals over photorealism. Backgrounds and scenarios may include objects recognizable from the real world, but they only need enough realistic detail to be understood. Treat those objects as part of the game's fantastical world rather than aiming for photographic rendering.
