* Shape character: A player owned character. Their bodies are geometrical shapes with floating hands and feet.
* Playground Lobby: The main menu of Play Shapes. A place where players gather after joining the game. Here they can interact with the scenario and each other until the host start a game mode.
* Minigames: Play shapes main gameplay. Each minigame is unique but they should share the same building blocks, as maximum as possible. We want to ensure that the game feel consistent throughout many different types of gameplay mechanics.
